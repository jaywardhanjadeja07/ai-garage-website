Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 23, 2025

Introduction

GarageAI ("we", "our", or "us") operates the GarageAI mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

1. Personal Information

We may collect the following personal information when you register and use the App:

  • Email address
  • Name
  • User account credentials

2. Vehicle Information

To provide diagnostic services, we collect:

  • Vehicle diagnostic data (OBD-II scan results, error codes)
  • Vehicle make, model, and year
  • Maintenance history

3. Location Information

With your permission, we collect:

  • Precise location data to help you find nearby garages and service centers
  • Location information is only collected when you use the garage finder feature

4. Usage Data

We automatically collect:

  • App usage statistics
  • Diagnostic scan history
  • Feature interaction data
  • Device information (device type, operating system version)

5. Advertising Data

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • Ad interaction data

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

  • Providing Services: Delivering vehicle diagnostic results and recommendations
  • Garage Network: Connecting you with partner garages and service centers
  • Parts Marketplace: Facilitating parts purchases through our marketplace
  • Referral Program: Managing the referral system and awarding free searches
  • Monetization: Displaying relevant advertisements through Google AdMob
  • Improvement: Analyzing app usage to improve features and user experience
  • Communication: Sending service updates and important notifications
  • Security: Protecting against fraud and unauthorized access

How We Share Your Information

Third-Party Service Providers

  • Google AdMob: For advertising (Privacy Policy)
  • Cloud Storage Providers: For secure data storage
  • Analytics Services: For app performance monitoring

Partner Garages

When you request service, we share necessary vehicle information with selected garages.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or to:

  • Comply with legal processes
  • Protect our rights and property
  • Prevent fraud or security issues
  • Protect user safety

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Secure authentication systems
  • Regular security updates
  • Access controls and monitoring

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Privacy Rights

Access and Control

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data
  • Opt-out of marketing communications

Location Permissions

You can control location access through your device settings. Disabling location will limit garage finder functionality.

Advertising Choices

You can:

  • Opt-out of personalized ads through your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads)
  • Reset your advertising ID
  • Limit ad tracking

Children's Privacy

GarageAI is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the App. AdMob uses cookies and similar technologies to provide personalized ads based on your interests.

Control AdMob Data:

  • Visit Google's Ad Settings: https://adssettings.google.com/
  • Opt-out of personalized advertising
  • Learn more: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
